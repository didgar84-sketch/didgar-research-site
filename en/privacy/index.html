Privacy and consultation information

Learn how consultation details prepare a WhatsApp message, how external services are used, and how to handle sensitive data or contact the institute.

Information used

You enter your name, contact, service, degree, deadline and topic in your browser. The API validates those details and generates a WhatsApp link. This version has no database, user accounts or permanent request records.

External services

The institute does not receive the request until you open WhatsApp and send the message. WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram policies apply when you leave this website. Persian and Arabic fonts are hosted on this website.

Sensitive data and retention

This form is not intended for sensitive raw data, participant identities or confidential documents. Agree a secure process before transferring these. The site uses no analytics cookies or advertising trackers; hosting providers may retain technical access logs under their own policies.
