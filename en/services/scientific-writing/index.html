Dr. Didgar Research Institute

Thesis and article writing consultation

Support for thesis structure, literature synthesis and scientific reporting, with clear deliverables and respect for authorship and institutional policies.

Research services

  • Introduction, problem statement, and research significance
  • Comprehensive literature review and background
  • Research methodology and study design
  • Results analysis and discussion
  • Conclusions, recommendations, and limitations
  • Abstract in multiple languages with keywords
  • ISI and peer-reviewed scientific articles
  • Textbook and specialized reference authoring

Expected deliverables

  • Agreed scope and work plan
  • Editable files or code appropriate to the service
  • Methods, assumptions and decision documentation
  • Review notes and usage guidance

What to prepare

  • Question, discipline and current research stage
  • Institutional or journal requirements and deadline
  • Existing manuscript or permitted de-identified data
  • Expected deliverables and supervisor comments
Scope, fees, tools, timing, revisions and support are agreed in writing. Researchers retain responsibility for understanding and approving the work.

Our process

  1. Review your topic, degree, available data and deadline.
  2. Agree deliverables, timeline and a transparent quotation.
  3. Review milestone outputs and progress reports.
  4. Check methods, analysis, sources and writing quality.
  5. Receive editable files, code and agreed documentation.
