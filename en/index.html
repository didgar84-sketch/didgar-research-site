Your questions. Clear answers.

Where should I start?

Choose a learning path matching your stage. Establish the question and design before measurement and analysis. The reading order does not mean waiting until after results to preregister.

Should I cite the guide or the original source?

These are educational interpretations with fictional examples. For a scientific claim, read and cite the relevant document you actually consulted. A list of links does not replace source reading.

Are the example data and findings real?

No. The executable records are entirely synthetic and are not findings about actual students. Teaching flags, TOST bounds and study plans require fresh justification for a real project.

Have the guides had independent specialist peer review?

That is not claimed for this edition. Sources and technical checks are documented and AI assistance disclosed. Methods, translations and complex health/rights/statistical decisions need appropriate specialist review for the actual case.

How are costs and turnaround determined?

A technical proposal follows review of the topic, degree level, data volume, methods and deadline. One price or turnaround cannot fit every project.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Your field, degree, topic, current stage, institutional requirements, available data and deadline. Do not send participant identifiers before agreeing a secure transfer process.

Which languages are available?

Content and consultation requests are available in English, Persian and Arabic. Deliverable languages and translation requirements are defined in the engagement.

Do you guarantee journal acceptance or grades?

No. Institutions, reviewers and editors make independent decisions. Support focuses on sound methods, analysis, learning, editing and documentation.

Does a similarity score prove plagiarism?

No. Matched sources, quotations, citations and report settings require contextual review. Your institution or journal sets its own requirements.

What are the support and revision terms?

Revision limits, support duration, available review tools and refund terms are defined in writing after the engagement scope is agreed.

Who is responsible for the research?

The researcher must understand and approve methods, data and the final manuscript, and disclose support under institutional or journal policy. Fabricating data or misrepresenting authorship is unacceptable.
