Open access and publishing rights: versions and repositories

Check open-access routes, article versions, embargoes, licenses and costs in official policies; free reading does not imply unrestricted reuse or universal repository eligibility.

Separate access from reuse

Free reading does not automatically permit translation, figure reuse or commercial redistribution. Check the actual license and agreement. Preprints, accepted manuscripts and publisher versions can have different conditions.

Compare routes and costs

Consider open-access journal publication and depositing an allowed version in a repository. Some journals charge and others do not; openness alone establishes neither price nor quality. University publisher agreements, including Oxford’s arrangements, do not automatically cover all researchers.

Check repository eligibility

Institutional repositories such as Apollo and OpenUCT preserve their communities’ outputs. Review membership, accepted versions, file formats and embargo rules. Reading a repository’s contents does not confer the right to deposit there.

Evaluate the publication venue

Verify publisher identity, peer review, fees, contact details and correction policies. DOAJ helps discover open-access journals but does not replace assessment of scientific fit. Familiar branding and rapid acceptance promises are insufficient evidence of trustworthiness.

Keep a rights record

Preserve the accepted version, agreement, license, deposit date, funding statement and publication identifier. Verify the terms for each reused figure or file. Clearly identify versions and peer-review status when listing your work.

Practical research checklist

  • Identify the version and license.
  • Check fees and possible support.
  • Verify repository deposit conditions.
  • Describe review status accurately.

For the fictional paper, compare repository access to an accepted manuscript with open access to the publisher version. Free reading does not imply no author charge, and viewing a free file does not establish reuse permission.
