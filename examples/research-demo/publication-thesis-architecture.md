# Fictional publication-thesis architecture

Local institutional rules determine eligibility, manuscript status, contributions
and permissions. This is a planning example, not a permitted universal format.

Central question: how do measurement, support use and reported experience inform
the interpretation of participation in a specific educational setting?

| Component | Role in the argument | Link and evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrated introduction | Defines the question, constructs and bounded contribution | One terminology set and an evidence map |
| Paper/chapter 1 | Examines measurement | Actual evidence and limitations; not assumed validation |
| Linking section | Moves from measurement to an association question | Explain why definitions permit the next question |
| Paper/chapter 2 | Estimates association under stated assumptions | Units, selection, uncertainty and limits |
| Linking section | Identifies what association does not explain | Introduce the need for an experience-based inquiry |
| Paper/chapter 3 | Examines reported experiences | Approach, reflexivity and permitted excerpts |
| Integrated discussion | Explains agreement, contrasts and overall contribution | No synthesis beyond evidence; specify generality |

Completed linking example: “The preceding chapter examined how participation was
operationalized. The next chapter estimates its association with support use,
addressing the second part of the central question. Different sampling and design
conditions constrain direct coefficient comparisons. The subsequent qualitative
chapter examines processes the association model cannot establish.”

Maintain paper version/status, contributor roles, coauthor agreement, figure/text
reuse permissions and thesis-deposit conditions. The documents above do not
represent actual publications. Consult the institution and ANU's architecture
guidance rather than adopting a fixed worldwide paper count.
