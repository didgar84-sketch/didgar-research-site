# Mock Stage 1 protocol exercise

**Fictional example. No actual registration, ethics approval, review or IPA.**
This outline is an independent writing exercise; the 12-record code demonstration
is not the proposed study and cannot supply its power calculation.

## Completed question and design paragraph

We propose estimating the association between academic-support use and a defined
participation score over one semester among eligible adult course participants.
This observational design does not identify a causal intervention effect. Baseline
motivation, measurement timing, sampling and potential selection require explicit
design review before collection. The primary estimand is the support coefficient
in the prospectively specified model, with population and units stated.

## Measurement and sampling decisions

Choose a permitted instrument with suitable measurement evidence. Record its
version, language, score derivation and timing. Define recruitment, inclusion,
exclusion and the independent observation unit. The illustrative recruitment
arithmetic 100/(1−0.15)=117.647, rounded to 118, only inflates an assumed analyzable
need; it does not justify that need. Replace the assumption with a reviewed
design-specific precision/power calculation and sensitivity scenarios.

## Analysis commitments to complete before results

Specify model formula, adjustment rationale, missingness, exclusions, multiplicity,
diagnostics, effect estimates and uncertainty. Identify exploratory analyses.
State all permitted branches and dependencies rather than choosing after results.
Preserve code, software versions, consent-compatible sharing and an immutable plan.

## Required evidence before a real submission

- Actual prior-data-access disclosure and no invented baseline findings.
- Permissions, ethics and consent decisions appropriate to the actual design.
- Completed and justified sample calculation and resources/timeline.
- The selected journal's current Stage 1 rules and any limits on prior access.
- Conditions, deadlines and amendments **only after an actual decision**.

This outline intentionally identifies decisions that need project-specific
evidence. It is not a finished submission or an official checklist. See COS
Registered Reports guidance and the related trilingual guide/workbook.
