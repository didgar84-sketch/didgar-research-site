## Completed example

| Stage/decision | Example | Check or action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question | Support–participation association in one semester | Are population, timing and variables explicit? |
| Measure | Permitted instrument with relevant evidence | Are definitions and measurement quality documented? |
| Design | Observational, without a causal-effect claim | Do title and aim match the design? |
| Analysis | Model suited to score and class structure | Have assumptions, missingness and clustering been assessed? |
| Feasibility | Permissions, access and fallback | Is the project executable before collection? |
